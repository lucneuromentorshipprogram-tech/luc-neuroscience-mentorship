03 / OUR MISSION

Make neuroscience feel more human.

We build meaningful peer relationships that help Loyola neuroscience students navigate college with greater confidence, belonging, and direction.

WHAT WE DO

Support that shows up in real life.

Peer mentorship. We pair students across class years so advice comes from someone who has recently walked the same path.

Community events. Low-pressure gatherings help members build friendships, trade experiences, and feel at home in the major.

Academic guidance. Mentors share practical perspective on courses, study habits, campus opportunities, and planning ahead.

Research connection. We help students become more comfortable exploring labs, faculty research, and undergraduate opportunities.

01 / CONNECT

Find your people

Tell us what you’re studying, what you’re curious about, and what kind of support would be useful.

02 / LEARN

Share what you know

Mentors offer practical perspective on classes, research, opportunities, and the everyday student experience.

03 / GROW

Keep moving forward

Build confidence, discover opportunities, and develop a network that lasts beyond one semester.
