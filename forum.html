07 / ASK A QUESTION

Ask the neuroscience community.

Have a question about courses, research, pre-health, study strategies, campus life, or finding your people? Submit it here. Your question goes into a private club database for the e-board to review and use in end-of-year program evaluation.

Question form

What can we help with?

Why we're collecting this

Turn questions into better programming.

Each submission can help the club understand what students actually need. The e-board dashboard can summarize volume by topic, role, class year, and semester, while keeping the full question text private to authorized club users.

Privacy: This form is designed for student support and program evaluation, not for collecting clinical or highly sensitive information. For formal human-subjects research or publication, the club should use Loyola's applicable review/approval process before treating these submissions as research data.
